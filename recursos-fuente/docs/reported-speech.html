Usamos el reported speech (estilo indirecto) para contar lo que alguien ha dicho. Para pasar de lo que se dijo a lo que contamos hay que cambiar tres cosas: el verbo para introducirlo (said, told, asked), los pronombres y, casi siempre, el tiempo verbal.

“I like doughnuts,” said Mabel.
Mabel said (that) she liked doughnuts.

Ponemos un verbo como said, cambiamos I por she y pasamos el present simple a past simple. El that es opcional.

Reported questions

Preguntas con who, what, when, where, how…

Usamos asked, mantenemos la palabra interrogativa y ponemos el orden de una frase normal (sujeto + verbo). No hay signo de interrogación ni do/did.

“What time is it?” asked Roberto. → Roberto asked what time it was.
“What time does the train leave?” asked Andrea. → Andrea asked what time the train left.
“When are you going to Granada?” asked Inma. → Inma asked when I was going to Granada.

Preguntas de sí o no

Empezamos igual, con asked, y añadimos if (o whether).

“Do you want to come to the supermarket with me?” asked Harriet. → Harriet asked if I wanted to go to the supermarket with her.
“Will you see your brother next week?” asked Leopold. → Leopold asked if I would see my brother the following week.
“Did you watch anything good on TV last night?” asked Hernán. → Hernán asked if I had watched anything good on TV the night before.

Reported orders

Para órdenes y peticiones usamos told o asked + persona + to + infinitivo. Si la orden es negativa, not to.

“Sit down!” said the teacher. → The teacher told us to sit down.
“Close the window, I'm freezing,” said Linzi. → Linzi told me to close the window because she was freezing.
“Don't be late,” said my boss. → My boss told me not to be late.

Ejercicios

1. Completa con el verbo en el tiempo correcto

  1. “I like chocolate,” she said. → She said that she (like) chocolate.
  2. “We are watching a film,” they said. → They said that they (watch) a film.
  3. “I visited Paris last year,” John said. → John said that he (visit) Paris the previous year.
  4. “I can speak three languages,” Maria said. → Maria said that she (can) speak three languages.
  5. “I am very tired,” he said. → He said that he (be) very tired.
  6. “I have finished my homework,” Anna said. → Anna said that she (finish) her homework.
  7. “I will call you tomorrow,” Tom said. → Tom said that he (will) call me the next day.
  8. “I love playing football,” Mark said. → Mark said that he (love) playing football.
  9. “I don't like coffee,” she said. → She said that she (not like) coffee.
  10. “I went to the supermarket yesterday,” Paul said. → Paul said that he (go) to the supermarket the day before.

2. Completa las preguntas en estilo indirecto

  1. “Where do you live?” she asked. → She asked me where I (live).
  2. “What are you doing?” he asked. → He asked me what I (do).
  3. “Did you see the film?” John asked. → John asked me if I (see) the film.
  4. “Can you help me?” Maria asked. → Maria asked me if I (can) help her.
  5. “Have you finished your homework?” Anna asked. → Anna asked me if I (finish) my homework.
  6. “Why are you late?” Tom asked. → Tom asked me why I (be) late.
  7. “Will you come to the party?” Mark asked. → Mark asked me if I (come) to the party.

3. Completa las órdenes y peticiones

  1. “Close the door,” she said. → She told me (close) the door.
  2. “Don't be late,” he said. → He told me (not be) late.
  3. “Sit down,” the teacher said. → The teacher told us (sit) down.
  4. “Please help me with this,” Maria said. → Maria asked me (help) her with that.
  5. “Stop talking!” the coach said. → The coach told us (stop) talking.

4. Reescribe cada frase en estilo indirecto

  1. “I love learning English,” he said.

  6. “How long have you been studying Spanish?” he asked.
  7. “I'm really tired today,” she said.
  8. “Please open the window,” he said to me.
  9. “Have you seen the new film yet?” she asked.
  10. “I will help you with your homework,” he said.
  11. “Why did you leave the party early?” she asked.
  12. “Remember to bring your passport,” he told me.
