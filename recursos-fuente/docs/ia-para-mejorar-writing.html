Un asistente de IA puede darte un feedback muy detallado sobre lo que escribes en inglés, pero solo si le das la información que necesita. Si le pegas el texto y le pides “corrígemelo”, te devolverá algo genérico. Si sigues estos tres pasos, te dará una corrección, una lista de errores y una nota para cada criterio.

1

Pega la tarea que te han pedido, para que sepa qué tenías que escribir.

2

Pega los criterios de evaluación de tu nivel, para que sepa cómo puntuarlo.

3

Pega tu texto y la instrucción final, para que sepa exactamente qué tiene que hacer.

Paso 1: la tarea

Copia el enunciado de tu tarea en el chat. Si no tienes una, aquí tienes un ejemplo para cada nivel.

Ejemplo de tarea · A2
Write an email to a new friend. Tell them about your family, where you live, and one thing you enjoy doing in your free time. Write 80 to 100 words.
Ejemplo de tarea · B1
You have seen this notice in an English-language magazine.
Stories wanted: A time I felt proud of myself. Tell us about a moment you felt proud. What happened? How did you feel? The best stories will appear in our next issue.
Write your story in 140 to 190 words.
Ejemplo de tarea · B2
You see this announcement on an English-language website.
Articles wanted: The most useful thing I have ever learned. What is the most useful thing you have learned? Who did you learn it from? Why is it useful?
Write your article in 140 to 190 words.
Ejemplo de tarea · C1
A magazine has asked for contributions to a series on modern life. Write a report examining whether people today have a better work-life balance than previous generations, referring to relevant evidence and giving your own conclusions. Write 220 to 260 words.
Ejemplo de tarea · C2
A respected journal is inviting submissions for a special edition on language and identity. Write an essay exploring the extent to which the language or languages we speak shape our sense of who we are. Draw on relevant examples and present a well-argued case. Write 280 to 320 words.

Paso 2: los criterios de tu nivel

Busca tu nivel y copia el bloque entero en el chat, debajo de la tarea. Los cuatro criterios son los mismos en todos los niveles: Content (¿respondes a todo lo que te piden?), Communication (¿el texto consigue su objetivo con el lector?), Organisation (¿está bien estructurado y conectado?) y Language (¿usas bien el vocabulario y la gramática?). Lo que cambia es lo que se exige en cada nivel.

Criterios A2 nota 3, 2 o 1

Copia y pega en el chat
Marking criteria (A2 level)

Content: Did the writer answer all parts of the task?
3: All key information is included. The reader is fully informed.
2: Most key information is included, with minor gaps.
1: Some information is missing or unclear. The reader is only partially informed.

Communication: Is the writing clear and easy to follow?
3: The writing is clear and appropriate for the task. Simple ideas are communicated effectively.
2: The writing is mostly clear, though occasionally unclear.
1: Some basic ideas are communicated, but the text may be difficult to follow.

Organisation: Is the text well organised?
3: The text is clearly organised, with basic linking words (and, but, because, so) used correctly.
2: There is some basic organisation, though it is not always consistent.
1: There is limited organisation. Ideas may feel disconnected.

Language: Are the vocabulary and grammar accurate?
3: Simple, familiar vocabulary is used correctly. Basic grammatical structures are mostly accurate.
2: Simple vocabulary is used with some errors that do not prevent understanding.
1: Very basic vocabulary and structures are used. Errors are frequent, but the meaning is partly clear.

5: Vocabulary is extensive, precise and used with full idiomatic naturalness. Grammatical structures are handled with complete mastery. Errors are minimal and do not affect communication.
3: A wide range of sophisticated vocabulary is used with precision and confidence. Complex grammatical forms are used with strong control and only occasional minor errors.
1: A good range of vocabulary and complex structures is used, though full precision and idiomatic naturalness may not always be achieved.

Cómo sacarle más partido: escribe un primer borrador, pide la corrección, reescríbelo tú sin copiar la versión corregida y vuelve a pedir la nota. Lo que aprendes está en esa segunda versión. Y ten en cuenta que la IA a veces se equivoca, así que si una corrección no te convence, pregúntale por qué.
