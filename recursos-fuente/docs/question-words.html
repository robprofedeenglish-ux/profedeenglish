What o which: usa which cuando eliges entre unas pocas opciones que tienes delante, y what cuando las opciones están abiertas. What's your favourite film? pero Which one do you want, this or that?

Ejercicio

Completa cada pregunta con who, what, where, when, why, which, how, how many o how much. Algunas no se usan.

  1. is knocking on the door?
  2. is your teacher's name?
  3. did you get here so quickly?
  4. are you doing this weekend?
  5. do you usually travel to school, by bus or on foot?
  6. is your best friend's favourite sport?
  7. do you get to the gym?
  8. are you talking to on the phone?
  9. is your favourite subject?
  10. books are there on the shelf?
  11. juice is there in the fridge?
  12. one do you prefer, the blue one or the black one?
  13. are you wearing a jacket? It's so hot!
  14. pets do you have at home?
  15. film do you want to see tonight?
  16. time do you have today?
  17. is your favourite teacher?

Soluciones

  1. Who
  2. What
  3. How
  4. What
  5. How
  6. What
  7. How
  8. Who
  9. What
  10. How many
  11. How much
  12. Which
  13. Why
  14. How many
  15. What (o Which, si eliges entre unas pocas)
  16. How much
  17. Who
